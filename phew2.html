More than massage. More than exercise.

Health and wellbeing rarely fit neatly into one category. Sometimes the body needs movement. Sometimes it needs rest. Sometimes it needs hands-on therapy. Sometimes it needs a different approach altogether. My role is to listen, assess and understand the bigger picture before developing a suitable plan.

Through massage therapy, movement, exercise referral and personal training, and wellness coaching, I aim to support people in understanding their bodies, improving functional movement, and working towards their individual goals.

Where appropriate, I also value working alongside other healthcare professionals, and would welcome the chance to build collaborative relationships with GP practices and other health professionals.

My approach

Professional assessment

A structured approach to assessment, treatment planning and record keeping, working within my professional scope and recognising when onward referral is the right next step.

Complementary

Massage and complementary therapies can be an important part of overall wellbeing. Treatments are individualised, not a one-size-fits-all routine.

Movement

The body is designed to move. Exercise referral, personal training and movement-based work build strength, mobility and a better understanding of your own body.

Education

Understanding is empowering. I tutor massage therapy at School of Bodywork in Exeter, helping students build the skills and awareness thoughtful practitioners need.

A lifetime of movement, challenges and learning

My relationship with movement began long before I ever considered it a career. Growing up in Berkshire, I was rarely far from a sports field — athletics, football, netball, gymnastics, games with friends. In my twenties I discovered the gym, and bodybuilding became a huge part of my life for sixteen years: competition, unexpected opportunities, setbacks, and a great deal learned about the physical and psychological sides of performance. One highlight was becoming Ladies Heavyweight British Bodybuilding Champion for two consecutive years — though the title was only one part of the journey.

Following my competitive career I was given the opportunity to own a gym in Exeter, which became my home. Running the gym gave me another perspective on health and fitness — I was surrounded by people with different bodies, abilities, motivations and reasons for wanting to feel healthier. That experience still shapes the way I work today.

Wellness coaching & teaching

Coaching lets me look beyond a single session to the whole person; teaching lets me pass on knowledge and experience to developing therapists.

These may look like different areas of work, but they're connected by the same philosophy: understand the individual, work with the person and not just the symptom, educate where possible, recognise limitations, and keep learning.

  • Managing everyday aches and muscular tension
  • Improving mobility and movement
  • Developing strength and physical confidence
  • Supporting an active lifestyle
  • Returning to exercise after periods of reduced activity
  • Maintaining wellbeing through distinct phases of life
  • Experiencing pregnancy and the postnatal period
  • Receiving tailored massage therapy
  • Developing knowledge and skills as a massage therapist

I also welcome the opportunity to work collaboratively with GPs, healthcare professionals and other appropriate referral networks, where my role can complement rather than replace medical care.

Ways I can help

Every treatment starts with listening. Open whichever fits where you are right now — each is adapted to your body, your stage of life and what you want to get out of it.

Massage therapy Targeted, individualised treatment to help you move, recover and feel more comfortable in your body

Massage therapy can help with muscular tension, everyday aches and pains, reduced mobility, the physical demands of an active lifestyle, or stress. Every treatment is tailored to you, taking into account how you feel, your lifestyle, and what you'd like to take away from it.

Whether you need support with recovery, chronic pain, or simply some time to relax and look after yourself, your treatment will be adapted to your individual needs.

Exercise referral & personal training Training for real life and a better quality of life, not just fitness goals or events

Exercise isn't only about losing weight, building muscle or preparing for an event. Sometimes it's about becoming stronger after a period of inactivity, managing changes in your health, improving your mobility, or simply feeling more capable in everyday life.

As an exercise referral fitness specialist and personal trainer, I take an individualised approach to training, working with you to understand where you are now and build a programme around your needs, abilities and aims.

No judgement. No pressure to keep up. Just safe, effective, purposeful exercise designed for you.

Prenatal & postnatal fitness training and massage Specialist support for your body through pregnancy, recovery and beyond

Pregnancy and the postnatal period can bring significant physical and emotional changes. Your body needs support that adapts with you — not a generic programme, or your current one carried on unchanged.

Training is tailored to your ability, your stage of pregnancy or recovery, helping you maintain or regain strength, mobility and confidence while respecting the changes your body is experiencing.

Massage can also provide valuable time to relax, release muscular tension, and support your changing body.

Oncology massage A gentle, supportive approach to massage during and after cancer treatment

Cancer and its treatment can place significant physical and emotional demands on the body. Oncology massage takes a considered, individualised approach, adapting techniques and positioning to your circumstances.

The focus is on a calm and supportive treatment that prioritises comfort, relaxation and wellbeing, taking into account your treatment history and current needs — allowing you time to reconnect with your body and mind.

It's safe and beneficial to receive massage from a qualified oncology massage therapist.

Menopause fitness training Strength and movement support for a changing body

Menopause can affect far more than your hormones. Changes in strength, muscle mass, bone health, energy, sleep and confidence can all influence how you feel in your body and how you approach exercise.

Training at this stage isn't about pushing harder — it's about exercising intelligently and consistently, with a programme designed around your individual needs.

Together, we can use strength and fitness training to help you maintain physical capacity, support bone and muscle health, and feel stronger and more confident through menopause and beyond.

Search within yourself

For me, this isn't simply a motto — it represents curiosity, self-awareness and personal responsibility. I've spent much of my life challenging myself physically, learning through experience, and discovering that progress doesn't always follow a straight line. I encourage my clients and students to be curious about their own bodies, to understand their capabilities and limitations, and to recognise that sometimes the most valuable step forward begins with looking within.

Search within yourself. Understand your body. Move with purpose. Keep learning.
